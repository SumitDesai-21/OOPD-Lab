Assignment 4: Inheritance and method overriding
• In a Vehicle Management System, every vehicle has brand, fuel, max speed, wheels and methods:
displayInfo(), start(), stop().
• Manual vehicle inherits from Vehicle, has fuel = None, Auto vehicle inherits from Vehicle, has mileage.
• Any vehicle is either Manual vehicle or Auto vehicle.
• Bike inherits from Auto vehicles has wheels=2 and has some other attributes and methods like
displayInfo(), ride() etc.
• Car inherits from Auto vehicles has wheels=4 and has some other attributes and methods like displayInfo(),
honk() etc.
• Demonstrate polymorphism.
